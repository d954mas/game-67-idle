## What

net_ws_server admits at most PENDING_PER_PEER = 4 sockets per address before HELLO. Mobile carriers (CGNAT), schools and offices put many players behind one address, and a room restart makes every player reconnect at once, so the fifth and later players behind one address are refused. What they see depends on whether the client retries a refusal.

## Done when

- [ ] What a refused client does today is known (retry and backoff, or an error screen) in both online games.
- [ ] A burst of reconnects from one address (e.g. 32) all reach the room; the per-address guard still bounds a flood.
- [ ] A test covers the burst.
