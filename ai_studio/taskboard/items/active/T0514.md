## What

The shared room entry (features/online-room) counts every failed try alike: a 4006 refusal, a TCP drop, a refused connect while a restarted room is not listening yet. After three it shows the "room full" panel. A player whose network blipped, or who hit a room restart, is told the room is full.

## Done when

- [ ] The entry tells a full room (close 4006 / seat refusal) apart from an unreachable room (connect failure, drop before HELLO) and shows a matching panel and copy.
- [ ] Retry timing fits a room restart (the new room may take a few seconds to listen).
- [ ] A test covers both paths; both online games show the right panel (screenshot).
