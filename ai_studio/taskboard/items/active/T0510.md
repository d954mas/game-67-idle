## What

A probe saw 20 simultaneous loopback WebSocket connects stall 10-25 s now and then (some hit the client's 20 s connect timeout). The cause is unknown: listen backlog, accept pacing, the client's connect path or the test harness. Seen on Windows loopback only (candidate: libuv Windows accept/backlog); check Linux first.
