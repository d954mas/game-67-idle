## What

net_ws_server's accept bucket (max_clients per second, burst 2*max_clients) is global and is charged before any per-address check. One address that opens and closes sockets in a loop holds nothing but drains every accept, so honest dials, a room restart's reconnect wave included, are dropped at TCP and the client shows "room full" after three tries.

## Done when

- [ ] A test shows one address churning connects no longer blocks a second address from getting in.
- [ ] The per-address share is bounded without breaking a CGNAT venue reconnecting together.
